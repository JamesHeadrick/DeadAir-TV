    Your services

    Subscriptions you pay for, in priority order: when a show is on several, the one higher up is shown first.

      Where to watch

      Your services always come first. If a show isn't on any of them:

      Shows with nowhere left to watch are skipped when picking.

      Channels

      Channels come from the tags on your shows (edit those under All shows). Give one an emoji, or rename or delete it on every show at once.

        Watched & skipped

        Episodes you mark ✓ Watched or Skip won't be picked again for a while. Everyone has their own history.

        Users

        Admins can change settings and manage users. Viewers can pick episodes and keep their own watched history.
